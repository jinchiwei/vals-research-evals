## Scientific target and data boundary

The capability is producing a **correct and defensible research artifact**: cohort, endpoint, image/feature treatment, evaluation, and bounded conclusion. A scientifically valid null or inconclusive result can earn full credit. An impressive AUROC from a leaked split or wrong endpoint cannot. Clinical usefulness of MRI-to-mutation prediction is a different research question and remains a secondary outcome.

The completed hosted audit confirms 150 imaging patients, the four advertised sequences, 64 genomic patients and 63 patients with the required FLAIR feature rows. It found no DSC/CBV sequence. Exactly five metadata/dictionary files and 33 staged FLAIR summaries match Box byte-for-byte; this does not establish whole-release equivalence or external validation. The archive includes metadata that must stay outside model packages. Source records and predictions remain on FAC; no frontier model received them. [Hosted acquisition/audit report](../../pcnsl/results/20261114-hosted-release/REPORT.md).

The fixed public-source baseline used 63 patients and ten spatial fractions, with train-fold-only imputation/scaling and fixed logistic regression. Mean pooled out-of-fold AUROC across three seeds was **0.6684 for MYD88** and **0.4434 for CD79B**. The endpoint is reported qualifying alteration versus no qualifying report, not verified mutant versus biological wild type. These descriptive internal-CV values neither establish clinical utility nor demonstrate an agent failure; seed ranges are not confidence intervals. [Baseline details and limitations](../../pcnsl/results/20261114-hosted-release/REPORT.md).

Keep three evidence tracks distinct:

- **Synthetic exact-reference tasks:** known labels, chronology conventions, image phantoms and software faults. Available now without sending patient records to model services. They test clinically motivated research behavior, not real PCNSL predictive accuracy.
- **Controlled scientific tasks:** known null/confounded/signal-generating processes. Grade the analysis warranted by the finite sample; do not require every sample to recover the latent truth or attain significance.
- **Authentic hosted-release tasks:** the archive audit and fixed baseline are complete; task-specific endpoint adjudication and a reviewed model-facing extract remain necessary. Freeze hashes, dictionary versions and inclusion rules. Unverified dates, assays, outcomes and modalities cannot become hidden answer keys.

## Original integrated-audit design and implemented scope

The design below motivated the implemented task. The actual version supplies a normative protocol and deterministic output contract; it does not implement every proposed open-ended research or expert-judging feature. Both tested models passed it, including its clean counterpart and held-out seed.

**Mission:** “Assess whether this PCNSL imaging–genomic result is supported. Make the repository reproducible, repair consequential errors you can establish, and produce the strongest defensible conclusion supported by the supplied evidence.” A short deliverable schema specifies code, cohort ledger, metrics and evidence references, without telling the agent which functions or claims are wrong.

**Inputs:** a small runnable repository; controlled study export; data dictionary and release notes; a plausible draft result; selected primary-source excerpts with bibliographic context; documented environment and compute allowance. For the first integrated candidate, use synthetic records and phantoms. The apparent conclusion should depend on two to four interacting mechanisms, not 30 unrelated traps.

**Outputs:** runnable patch; patient/episode/assay inclusion ledger; explicit endpoint specification; split and fitted-transform provenance; saved predictions where applicable; numerical report; claim-to-evidence table; explanation of remaining uncertainty. “Not supported” requires identifying what is missing or wrong and preserving the valid portions of the work.

**Controls:** an otherwise similar clean repository, a reference repair, a naive reproduce-only baseline, and independent hidden fixtures. A blanket-refusal baseline and a blanket-approval baseline must both perform poorly. The reference solution is executed and checked before testing a frontier system. The same scientific contract and tool budget apply to flawed and clean cases.

## Reference standard, scoring and scientific validity

Each task has an independently authored reference implementation plus an evidence rubric specifying acceptable alternatives. Exact computational facts use deterministic tests: labels under a declared policy, cohort cardinality, physical quantities, split membership, transform fitting boundaries, recalculated metrics, and executable replay. Analyze several scientifically defensible choices as an equivalence class; do not require the reference author's favorite model or wording.

Claims and ambiguous study-design choices need domain review. Two reviewers should independently judge all alleged severe failures and a sample of passes/controls, blinded to model identity; disagreements remain unresolved until adjudicated. An LLM judge can help locate evidence but is not sole authority for the headline failure count. Report computational correctness, scientific validity, reproducibility, claim support and unnecessary refusal separately. Mark critical errors such as unsupported labels or leakage explicitly; prose quality cannot cancel them.

PROBAST+AI distinguishes evaluation bias from development quality and applicability; TRIPOD+AI/CLAIM support transparent reporting rather than declaring one algorithm correct. Somatic variant guidance and IBSI motivate endpoint and image-feature contracts. These standards inform task authoring but do not create a real-cohort oracle without verified source evidence. See the [domain review's standards](pcnsl-literature.md) and [evaluation review's methodology](evaluation-literature.md).

## Isolation and contamination: what is and is not guaranteed

The evaluated agent must not receive reference solutions, hidden fixtures, graders, developer notes, previous successful submissions or other attempts. Build contexts must include only the intended model-visible material. Grader and solution files copied into the root of a task image would defeat “hidden tests” even if the prompt never names them.

**Current limitation:** the grader/root runtime is trusted. Hiding an oracle by path convention, mounting it later, or running a root-owned grader is not an adversarial security proof. A root-capable agent in the same accessible container can potentially inspect or alter materials unless the runtime enforces separation. The exporter’s root/container path remains untested at this snapshot. Before claiming oracle isolation, inspect the built image, mount timing, privileges, writable paths, and replay phase in the actual selected provider; test that the agent cannot list/read the withheld material. Prefer a separate trusted verifier environment and fresh replay with only the submitted artifact and allowed inputs. Record the residual trust boundary rather than labeling the harness secure by assumption.

Also distinguish **patient leakage** in the scientific pipeline, **answer leakage** through the task environment, and **training contamination** from public data/papers. Hidden seeds alone address none of these completely. Public release identifiers, filenames and prose can make familiar cases recognizable. New prompts do not prove contamination freedom. Freeze data/task hashes; withhold independently authored task mechanisms or compositions; publish the exact overlap and retrieval policy. Do not inspect final holdouts while adapting the generator and continue calling them untouched.

## Bounded experiment loop

1. Validate one integrated candidate and its clean counterpart with independent references and naive baselines. Confirm the task is scientifically answerable and the grader catches the intended mechanism.
2. Calibrate tool/time allowance against reference computation and expert completion. Start with enough time for at least two complete inspect-edit-run cycles; avoid creating difficulty through missing packages or arbitrary time pressure. Record CPU/RAM, context, reasoning configuration and actual tool access.
3. Run development instances on the two available frontier configurations. Record every success, substantive mistake, timeout and infrastructure failure. Current all-pass results remain visible. Change one hypothesis at a time and version the task/generator.
4. Freeze prompt, generator, verifier, budget and allowed inputs. Test unseen structural fixtures; label this within-family generalization. Reserve a separately authored mechanism/composition to support a broader held-out-family claim.
5. Adjudicate and reproduce any proposed failure. A corrected result after a documented self-check is an intervention outcome, not grounds to erase the original attempt. A grader defect invalidates that measurement; it is not an agent error.
6. Launch an oracle smoke task and then a frontier task **through Valkyrie** once access is available. Save Tracker run ID, immutable dataset version, task IDs, agent/model versions, trace, terminal rewards and outputs. Local pilot results remain local until this occurs.
7. Stop or pivot at the predeclared time/spend cap. If the integrated task also passes, report a useful capability/control result and investigate a new validated mechanism; do not manufacture a failure by hiding necessary evidence or demanding impossible mutation prediction.

Repeated attempts measure stochastic reliability, not additional independent clinical patients or independent scientific discoveries. Report per-family/control counts first. With few independently authored families, avoid a precise population failure-rate claim. Cluster uncertainty by genuine task family/source case when enough clusters exist; do not treat hundreds of correlated CV outputs as hundreds of benchmark trials.

## Valkyrie and the demo gate

Official Valkyrie supports hosted datasets and a local Tracker with Docker. The SDK's archive validation is not execution; a local native-CLI pilot is not a Tracker run. Hosted Tracker requires a published dataset rather than a filesystem folder. A local Docker oracle can run without model credentials but requires the service/runtime stack, currently absent on the inspected teal PATH. Hosted provider/model access is pending; the user has requested preparation while awaiting credits. Exact commands and prerequisite checks are in [valkyrie-integration.md](valkyrie-integration.md), based on [official source](https://github.com/vals-ai/valkyrie).

The minimum honest demo shows one coherent research question, the plausible initial result, a recorded frontier mistake, the executable check establishing why it is wrong, the reference repair, and success on a matched clean control. It also shows the Valkyrie run evidence and states whether inputs are synthetic or an audited hosted-release extract. If a substantive failure or actual framework run is still missing, show the preparation and its limits rather than presenting a hypothetical trace as a completed benchmark.

## Novelty and claims we will not make

[BLADE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.09667) already evaluates scientific analytical decisions against expert analyses. [SciIntegrity-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10246) studies honest failure under completion pressure. [ReplicatorBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11354) includes positive and negative scientific replication cases. [PAPERBENCHX](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39744) evaluates regenerated scientific evidence after replay. Broader analysis/ML engineering benchmarks already cover tool-driven experiments and data pipelines. The project is not the first scientific-agent, integrity, or replay benchmark.

The intended contribution remains narrower: clinical imaging–genomic semantics integrated with executable repair and matched controls, eventually demonstrated on Valkyrie with a verified frontier failure. The present version has not achieved that gap. Direct PCNSL MRI–genotype studies already exist, and the literature search was not exhaustive. The defensible current claim is **a validated local research-audit control suite, completed hosted-data audit and descriptive baseline, with all tested frontier attempts passing and platform execution pending**.
